      A NOTE IN THE MARGIN
      Evidence you can inspect.
      Citation key collisions
      Keys compared after lowercasing. A matching key is a naming conflict, even when the publications differ.
      Literal DOI matches
      Resolver and doi: prefixes are removed, then ASCII identifier text is lowercased. No DOI lookup or publication verification.
      Plain title + year
      Braces, case and whitespace normalized. Requires a literal title without TeX commands and a four-digit year. No fuzzy matching or accent conversion.
      Made for small, local reviews. Your source never leaves this page; nothing is saved in browser storage. No PDFs, attachments, reference-manager libraries, macros or external URLs are opened. Full scanner limits.
